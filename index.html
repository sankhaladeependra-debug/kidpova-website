For parents

Built around what parents worry about

Most kids' apps are made to keep children tapping. Kidpova is made to put you in charge: how long your child plays, what they practise, and what they are really learning.

  • “Bas paanch minute aur…” Taking the phone away turns into a fight.

    The app ends play, so you don't have to

    Choose a play time from 5 to 60 minutes. Pova gives a one-minute warning, gets sleepy, says goodbye, and the app closes itself. Games stay closed for a rest time you pick (5 to 60 minutes). There is no “one more round” button.

    For you: fewer tantrums, and you are not the bad guy.

  • “Apps teach what they want, not what my child needs.”

    You choose what your child learns

    In ABC Balloons, switch on only the topics you want, out of 12: letters, numbers, colours, shapes, animals, fruits, food, vehicles, body parts, clothes, things at home and everyday objects. Focus on letters before school, or keep it to animals and colours for a little one. Food stays vegetarian unless you turn on egg and chicken.

    For you: practice that matches your goals and your family's values.

  • “Is my child learning, or just tapping?”

    See what your child really knows

    The progress page for grown-ups shows each topic: what your child knows well, what is getting there, what they are still practising, and what they mix up, like b and d. Something counts as “knows well” only after your child finds it on the first try, again and again, on more than one day, so lucky guesses don't count. You also see what they found today and what was new.

    For you: you know where your child stands without quizzing them, and what to practise together at home.

  • “Too hard and they give up. Too easy and they get bored.”

    Right for their age from the first tap

    Pick your child's age and ABC Balloons sets itself up. At 2: two big, slow balloons that wait in the air, extra hints and six-minute sessions. At 5: more balloons, quicker rounds and a small mission each day. If the age you set seems too hard, the game quietly plays a step younger. After that, levels move with how your child plays, not their age.

    For you: no frustration, no boredom, and nothing to set up.

  • “Stickers and streaks get kids hooked.”

    Rewards only if you want them

    Stickers, medals and gifts are off until a grown-up turns them on. No streaks, no ads, no pop-ups. Settings sit behind a parental gate, so only you can change them.

    For you: your child plays to learn, not to collect.

  • “One phone, two children.”

    A profile for each child

    Up to 6 children, each with their own animal face, age, levels and progress. No login, no account: it all stays on your phone.

    For you: siblings share the phone, not their progress.

Personalized difficulty

Games that meet your child where they are

No levels to grind, no pressure and no “fail” screens. The games quietly get easier or harder based on how your child plays.

  1. Your child plays

    Short, calm games with Pova's voice guiding each step.

  2. The app notices

    What your child gets right the first time, and where they slip.

  3. The next round adjusts

    A step harder when it's easy, quietly easier when it's hard, so play stays just right.

What needs practice comes back more often, and what your child already knows returns a few days later so it sticks. If two things get mixed up, like M and W, Kidpova practises them side by side. All of this happens on the phone, and you can see your child's progress in settings, behind a parental gate.

Meet Pova

Pova is a honey-golden baby puppy with a blue collar and a little star tag. Curious, gentle and patient, Pova plays alongside your child, notices their effort and celebrates trying, not just right answers. When a child makes a mistake, Pova says something like “That one is blue. Let's look again.”

What Pova never does:

  • Never looks sad or guilty when your child stops playing.
  • Never pressures with streaks or “just one more” prompts.
  • Never pretends to be a person.
  • Never hides behind rewards your child has to unlock.

Screen to life

Every session ends with real-world play

When a session is done, Pova waves goodbye and suggests one simple thing to do away from the screen, using what's already at home.

  • After Counting FruitsCount the spoons as you set the table.
  • After Color SplashFind something yellow on your walk.
  • After Animal SoundsMoo like a cow, then quack like a duck.

A note from our founder

Hi, I'm Deependra Sankhala. I'm a software developer, and a dad to a little girl.

When our daughter started reaching for the phone, I went looking for a learning app I could trust. I wanted short sessions, not endless screen time. I wanted her to learn new words and recognise the everyday things around our home. I wanted a warm, friendly voice, a way to see how she was doing, and no ads at all. Most apps I tried missed something on that list, so I started building Kidpova.

Kidpova fixes what bothered me most as a parent:

  • Your child stays in the game. A child lock keeps little hands inside Kidpova, so they don't wander off into other apps.
  • You decide the screen time. When it's up, Pova says a gentle goodbye, and your child puts the phone down on their own and goes off to play in the real world.
  • The game teaches at your child's pace. It levels up or down by itself, Pova guides your child when they get stuck, and you can see all of their progress in the app, offline.

My promise to parents: no ads, ever, and your child's data stays on your phone. Every game should teach something real, then send your child back to play in the real world.

— Deependra Sankhala, founder of Kidpova

Questions parents ask

What is Kidpova?

Kidpova is a learning-games app for children aged 2 to 5, made for families in India. Pova, a gentle, playful baby puppy, guides short games in Hindi or English. The games adjust to how your child plays, and each session ends with an idea for real-world play. Kidpova is coming soon to Android.

What ages is Kidpova for?

Kidpova is for children aged 2 to 5. Every instruction is spoken, so children who can't read yet can play on their own, with you nearby. For two-year-olds, short sessions played together with a grown-up work best.

Who is Pova?

Pova is Kidpova's mascot: a curious, gentle and playful baby puppy with a blue collar and a star tag. Pova speaks to children in Hindi or English, celebrates effort as well as right answers, and never pressures a child to keep playing.

Which games are in Kidpova?

Kidpova launches with 9 games: ABC Balloons, Letter Tracing, Varnamala Train, Counting Fruits, Shape Puzzle, Color Splash, Baby Piano, Animal Sounds and Magic Drawing. They practise English letters, Hindi letters (varnamala), early writing, counting, shapes, colors, listening, rhythm and creativity.

How does Kidpova adapt to my child?

Kidpova has a smart learning engine that runs entirely on the phone. It notes what your child gets right the first time and where they slip. When something is easy, the level goes up gently; when it's hard, it quietly gets easier, and there are no “fail” screens. If your child gets stuck, Pova shows them what to do. What needs practice comes back more often, what your child knows is repeated a few days later, and look-alike pairs such as M and W get extra practice. Parents can see progress in settings, behind a parental gate.

Is Kidpova safe for young children?

Kidpova is built with young children in mind: a child lock stops little hands from leaving the app or opening other things, a time limit ends sessions gently, there are no ads, and Pova never uses streaks or “one more” prompts. For two-year-olds, we recommend short sessions played together with a grown-up.

Can I limit how long my child plays Kidpova?

Yes. Behind the parental gate, choose a play time from 5 to 60 minutes. Pova gives a one-minute warning, says goodbye, and the app closes itself. Games stay closed for a rest time you choose, from 5 to 60 minutes. Settings also show how long your child has played today.

Can I see what my child is learning in Kidpova?

Yes. The progress page for grown-ups, behind the parental gate, shows each topic's level, what your child knows well, what is getting there, what they are still practising, and which things they mix up, such as b and d. It also shows what they found today and what was new. It all stays on your phone.

Can I choose what my child learns in Kidpova?

Yes. In ABC Balloons you can switch on only the topics you want, out of 12: letters, numbers, colours, shapes, animals, fruits, food, vehicles, body parts, clothes, things at home and everyday objects. Food stays vegetarian unless you turn on egg and chicken. Stickers and other rewards are off until you turn them on.

Does Kidpova work offline?

Yes. Once Kidpova is installed, every game works without an internet connection. The app doesn't use the internet at all, and there is no login or account.

Which languages does Kidpova speak?

Pova speaks Hindi and English, and every instruction is voiced, so children don't need to read. A full Hindi version of this website is coming later.

How is Kidpova different from other learning apps?

Kidpova is designed to be calm: one thing moves at a time, there are no ads or streaks, and every session ends by sending your child off to play in the real world, with an idea that uses things already at home. Parents stay in charge: you set the play time and the app closes itself, you choose the topics, you see what your child knows and mixes up, and rewards stay off unless you turn them on. Games start at your child's age. Pova speaks Hindi and English.

Does Kidpova collect my child's data?

No. The Kidpova app has no login, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it asks for no permissions: not internet, camera, microphone or location. Settings and learning progress are saved only on your phone. You can clear them with “Start learning afresh” in settings, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

What data does the Kidpova website collect?

On this website, only your email and, if you choose, your child's age group, to tell you about the launch. No analytics, no cookies, no trackers. Read the privacy policy.

How much does Kidpova cost?
