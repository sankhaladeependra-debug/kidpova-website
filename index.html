Personalized difficulty

Games that meet your child where they are

No levels to grind and no pressure. The games quietly get easier or harder based on how your child plays.

  1. Your child plays

    Short, calm games with Pavo's voice guiding each step.

  2. The app notices

    It sees what feels easy and what feels hard right now.

  3. The next game adjusts

    A little more challenge, or a little more help, so play stays just right.

9 games at launch

Small games, real skills

Each game practises one skill and ends with an idea to try off screen. Shape Puzzle and Color Splash are smart games that adapt as your child plays.

  • Balloons

    Hand–eye coordination and colors

    Off screen: Bat a real balloon back and forth and keep it off the floor.

  • Train

    Matching and putting things in order

    Off screen: Line up cushions or chappals into a train and say “choo choo!”

  • Counting Fruits

    Counting and early numbers

    Off screen: Count the bananas or rotis together at mealtime.

  • Shape Puzzle Smart game

    Shapes and how pieces fit

    Off screen: Find a circle, a square and a triangle in your kitchen.

  • Color Splash Smart game

    Naming and sorting colors

    Off screen: Go on a hunt for three red things around the house.

  • Baby Piano

    Listening, rhythm and cause and effect

    Off screen: Tap a beat on steel bowls with a spoon and take turns copying it.

  • Animal Sounds

    Listening and animal names

    Off screen: Spot a crow, cow or dog outside and copy the sound it makes.

  • Magic Drawing

    Creativity and finger control

    Off screen: Draw the same picture with a finger in a tray of flour or rice.

  • Letter Tracing

    Letter shapes and early writing

    Off screen: Trace the letter in sand, or on a steamy bathroom mirror.

Meet Pavo

Pavo is a baby penguin with a little sprout on their head. Curious, gentle and patient, Pavo plays alongside your child, notices their effort and celebrates trying, not just right answers. When a child makes a mistake, Pavo says something like “That one is blue. Let's look again.”

What Pavo never does:

  • Never looks sad or guilty when your child stops playing.
  • Never pressures with streaks or “just one more” prompts.
  • Never pretends to be a person.
  • Never hides behind rewards your child has to unlock.

Screen to life

Every session ends with real-world play

When a session is done, Pavo waves goodbye and suggests one simple thing to do away from the screen, using what's already at home.

  • After Counting FruitsCount the spoons as you set the table.
  • After Color SplashFind something yellow on your walk.
  • After Animal SoundsMoo like a cow, then quack like a duck.

Safety built in

Made for little hands

  • Child lock

    Little hands can't accidentally leave the app or open other things.

  • Gentle auto close

    Set a time limit. When it's up, the session ends calmly with a goodbye from Pavo.

  • Works offline

    Once installed, the games play without internet. Good for travel and patchy networks.

  • No ads

    No ads in the games, ever. Nothing pops up in the middle of play.

Questions parents ask

What is Kid Pavo?

Kid Pavo is a learning-games app for children aged 1 to 5, made for families in India. Pavo, a gentle baby penguin, guides short games in Hindi or English. The games adjust to how your child plays, and each session ends with an idea for real-world play. Kid Pavo is coming soon to Android.

What ages is Kid Pavo for?

Kid Pavo is for ages 1 to 5. For ages 1 to 2, it is made for playing together with a grown-up, side by side, for a few minutes at a time. Children aged 3 to 5 can play more on their own, with you nearby.

Who is Pavo?

Pavo is Kid Pavo's mascot: a curious, patient baby penguin with a little sprout on their head. Pavo speaks to children in Hindi or English, celebrates effort as well as right answers, and never pressures a child to keep playing.

Which games are in Kid Pavo?

Kid Pavo launches with 9 games: Balloons, Train, Counting Fruits, Shape Puzzle, Color Splash, Baby Piano, Animal Sounds, Magic Drawing and Letter Tracing. They practise colors, counting, shapes, listening, rhythm, creativity and early letter shapes.

How does Kid Pavo adapt to my child?

Kid Pavo has personalized difficulty. As your child plays, the app notices what feels easy or hard, and the next game gets a little easier or a little harder. Shape Puzzle and Color Splash are smart games that adapt as your child plays.

Is Kid Pavo safe for young children?

Kid Pavo is built with young children in mind: a child lock stops little hands from leaving the app or opening other things, a time limit ends sessions gently, there are no ads, and Pavo never uses streaks or “one more” prompts. For ages 1 to 2, we recommend playing together with a grown-up.

Does Kid Pavo work offline?

Yes. Once Kid Pavo is installed, the games work without an internet connection.

Which languages does Kid Pavo speak?

Pavo speaks Hindi and English, and every instruction is voiced, so children don't need to read. A full Hindi version of this website is coming later.

When does Kid Pavo launch?

Kid Pavo is coming soon to Android. Leave your email above and we'll send one message when it's ready.

Is Kid Pavo available on iPhone?

Kid Pavo is coming to Android first. We haven't announced other platforms yet.

How is Kid Pavo different from other learning apps?

Kid Pavo is designed to be calm: one thing moves at a time, there are no ads or streaks, and every session ends by sending your child off to play in the real world, with an idea that uses things already at home. Pavo speaks Hindi and English.

What data does the Kid Pavo website collect?

On this website, only your email and, if you choose, your child's age group, to tell you about the launch. No analytics, no cookies, no trackers. Read the privacy policy. We'll publish the app's privacy details before launch.

How much will Kid Pavo cost?

We'll share pricing before launch.
