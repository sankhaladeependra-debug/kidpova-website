Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Kid Pavo makes learning games for young children, so we take privacy seriously, for parents and for children. This page explains what the kidpavo.com website collects and why. The Kid Pavo app will have its own privacy details, published before launch.

What we collect

Only what you type into the “Notify me at launch” form:

  • Your email address (required), so we can tell you when the app launches.
  • Your child's age group (optional: 1–2, 3–4 or 5), so we know which ages are waiting. We never ask for a child's name, photo or any other detail about your child.
  • The date and time you signed up.

The form is for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Children should not use it.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, no cookies and no trackers on this website at this stage.
  • No advertising, and no data shared with advertisers.

How we use it

We use your email only to send launch updates about Kid Pavo. We don't sell, rent or share your details with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are saved in a Google Sheet owned by Kid Pavo, using Google's services (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets). Only the Kid Pavo team can see it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until Kid Pavo launches and for as long as you want updates. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details. Until we have a contact email, please use the sign-up form to request this, or once we have written to you, simply reply to any email from us. We will delete your details promptly.

Children's privacy and Indian law

Kid Pavo is built in India and we aim to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), including its extra care for children's data. On this website we collect no personal data from children. Before the app launches, we will explain how the app treats children's data and how parents give consent.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
